Who We Are

A home built on faith, care, and community

AGAPE — the Greek word for unconditional love — is more than our name. It is the foundation on which every child at our orphanage and school stands.

Located in the Sanskruti Township, Pocharam Village, we provide a safe, nurturing home to orphaned and underprivileged children. Our school equips every child with quality education, skills, and the confidence to step into the world.

We believe no child should grow up feeling unseen. Here, every child is known by name, celebrated on their birthday, and cheered on at every milestone.

What We Do

Programs that shape whole children, not just students

Home & Care

Safe Residential Home

Clean, comfortable living spaces with nutritious food, medical care, and a warm community. Every child has a bed, belongings, and belonging.

Learning

AGAPE School

Formal schooling from primary level with trained teachers, books, uniforms, and a curriculum that nurtures curiosity alongside knowledge.

Enrichment

Arts & Life Skills

Music, drawing, sports, cooking, and leadership programs that go beyond the classroom — growing confidence, creativity, and character.

Health

Medical & Wellness

Regular health check-ups, vaccinations, dental care, and emotional wellbeing support so every child grows up healthy in mind and body.

Future

Vocational Guidance

For older children — career counselling, interview preparation, and connections to higher education opportunities across Telangana.

Community

Volunteer & Outreach

Welcoming individuals, corporates, and groups to visit, teach, celebrate, and walk alongside our children in their journey.
